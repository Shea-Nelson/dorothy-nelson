# Dorothy Mae Nelson

A static family memorial for Dorothy Mae (Tharpe) Nelson (1922–2009), of Chipley and Panama City, Florida, wife of Milton Nelson and grandmother of Shea.

The site is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Open `index.html` in a browser, or visit the published copy:

https://shea-nelson.github.io/dorothy-nelson/

It is built to stand beside the memorial for her husband:

https://shea-nelson.github.io/milton-nelson-navy/

No public portrait of Dorothy is used here. The Family memories section is left ready for the family to fill, and will be labeled as memory rather than as a record. Facts are cited in the Sources section. What is still unconfirmed is listed under Open questions.

Compiled by the Nelson family, 2026.
