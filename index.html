A family memorial

Dorothy Mae Nelson

Born Dorothy Tharpe, in Chipley, Florida

Panama City · Springfield Baptist Church

July 8, 1922 – March 2, 2009

She was the wife of Milton Nelson, the mother of Randy, Ronnie, and Kenny, and the grandmother of Shea, who keeps this history. She is buried beside Milton at Evergreen Memorial Gardens. No photograph of her has been found in a public source that can be used here. This page does not pretend otherwise.

01 — Her story

From Chipley to Panama City

Dorothy Mae Nelson was born July 8, 1922, in Chipley, Florida. She died March 2, 2009, at the age of 86, of Panama City. Both dates, the birthplace, and her membership in Springfield Baptist Church are in the obituary published by Wilson Funeral Home and by the Panama City News Herald.

On August 16, 1944, she married Milton Nelson in Washington County, Florida. The county marriage index records her maiden name as Tharpe, and his as Nelson, in Marriage Book M, page 286. He was still in the Navy. Their later life together is told on his memorial.

She was preceded in death by her husband and by two sisters, named in her obituary as Oceal Creamer and Thedral Fowler. She was survived by two sisters, Myrtle Cook and her husband Rufus, and Bette Ausland; by a brother-in-law, John Nelson; and by two sisters-in-law, Betty Nelson and Montez Vincent and her husband Howard. Her three sons are named there as Randy Nelson and his wife Mary; Ronnie Nelson and his wife Marie; and Kenny Nelson and his wife Judy. Kenny’s full name is Robert Kenny Nelson. The obituary names seven grandchildren — Kenya, Keith, Shea, Jeremy, Jacob, Kyle, and Evan — and six great-grandchildren. No other details of living relatives are printed here.

Funeral services were held at 2 p.m. on Thursday, March 5, 2009, in the Wilson Funeral Home chapel, with the Rev. Jerry Everage officiating. Friends were received the evening before, from 6 to 8 p.m. Interment followed at Evergreen Memorial Gardens in Panama City, where her Find a Grave memorial stands beside Milton’s. This page links to that memorial and does not copy its photograph.

The obituary thanks the nursing staff at 3 Central at Bay Medical Center for their care. It does not say, in so many words, that she died at the hospital. This page does not add that.

02 — Her people

Parents and sisters

The public record of her parents is indirect, and the names do not all agree. What follows is only what the sources actually show.

Her 2009 obituary does not name her father or mother. It does name four sisters. Myrtle D. Cook, one of those sisters, died in 2023. Her obituary, also published by Wilson Funeral Home, says she was “the youngest of five daughters of General A. and Laura L. Tharp,” born near Chipley on June 17, 1935, and that the family moved to Panama City in 1950.

Five daughters, four sisters named beside Dorothy, and a Chipley beginning, line up. That is the best public identification of her parents this page has. Dorothy is not named in Myrtle’s obituary, and she is not linked as a daughter on General Tharp’s Find a Grave memorial. The identification still depends on that sister-to-sister match.

Unconfirmed The 1940 and 1950 census schedules for the Tharp household in Washington County were not retrieved for this page. They would be the ordinary place to see Dorothy in her parents’ home before her marriage, and to see how the enumerator wrote the family name.

The name of her mother is not settled in the sources. Myrtle’s obituary calls her Laura L. Tharp. A 1999 survey of Rock Hill Church Cemetery in Chipley, contributed to the USGenWeb Archives, lists, next to General Tharp (October 1, 1895 – October 30, 1965, with the marker wording “FL PVT CO 150 INFANTRY WWI”), a burial for Leola Tharp, October 11, 1900 – December 24, 1994. Find a Grave records that burial as Leola McDaniel Tharp and links her as General Tharp’s wife. The family has remembered the name as Leola. This page sets all three forms down and does not choose among them. The maiden name McDaniel comes from the Find a Grave memorial; a marriage record for General and Leola, or Laura, was not opened here.

The spelling of the family name also varies. The marriage index uses Tharpe for Dorothy. The same compiled indexes use Tharp for her sisters Oceal and Thedra. Myrtle’s obituary and the cemetery survey use Tharp.

The sisters, with the spellings as the sources give them:

  • Oceal Creamer. Preceded Dorothy in death. A Washington County marriage index records Oceal Tharp and Horace Creamer, October 2, 1944. An obituary for Oceal was not found for this page, and her dates of birth and death are not confirmed here.
  • Thedra C. Fowler. Dorothy’s obituary spells the name Thedral Fowler. A Washington County marriage index records Thedra C. Tharp and Robert Fowler, November 27, 1937. Find a Grave records Thedra C. Tharp Fowler, 1919–1998. The years are from that memorial, not from an obituary found for this page. The 1998 date is consistent with her having predeceased Dorothy.
  • Bette Ausland. The family has remembered the name as Betty. Find a Grave records Betty Lou Tharp Ausland, 1930–2011. She was living when Dorothy died in 2009. The 2011 year is from that memorial, not from an obituary found for this page.
  • Myrtle D. Cook. Born June 17, 1935, near Chipley; died June 28, 2023; buried at Evergreen Memorial Gardens. Her own obituary is the source for the “five daughters of General A. and Laura L. Tharp,” and for the family’s move to Panama City in 1950.

03 — Marriage and home

Mrs. Milton Nelson

The Washington County, Florida, marriage index records the marriage of Milton Nelson and Dorothy Tharpe on August 16, 1944, in Marriage Book M, page 286.

Milton Nelson was born November 8, 1922, and died December 23, 1997. He served in the United States Navy in the Second World War, was a plank owner of the attack transport USS Noble (APA-218), and finished as a coxswain. He is buried at Evergreen Memorial Gardens. That history, with its sources, is on his memorial.

The 1950 census, as already read for that memorial, finds him in Panama City, age 27, head of household, with his wife listed as Dorothy M. and two sons: Mitchell R., age 4, and Ronald E., age 2. The family remembers three sons: Randy, Ronnie, and Kenny. This page, like his, does not try to line the census names up with the names the family uses, beyond setting both down. The census schedule itself was not re-opened for this page.

The formal obituary calls her “of Panama City.” In the guest book published with that notice, neighbors and friends remembered her on the Millville Block and as a neighbor on Elm Avenue. Those are remembrances, not a city directory. They are the public source for the family’s memory that she lived in Millville, in the Panama City area.

07 — Still to confirm

Open questions

These are the facts this page would still like to settle. None of them is filled in by guessing.

  1. The 1940 and 1950 census schedules for the Tharp or Tharpe household in Washington County, Florida — and whether Dorothy appears in the 1940 household before her marriage.
  2. Her mother’s name: Laura L., as Myrtle’s obituary has it, or Leola, as the Rock Hill cemetery survey, the Find a Grave memorial, and the family’s memory have it. They may be the same woman. This page has not proved that.
  3. Whether McDaniel, the maiden name on the Find a Grave memorial for Leola Tharp, can be confirmed in a marriage record or other vital record.
  4. The spelling on the original marriage license in Washington County Marriage Book M, page 286: Tharpe or Tharp.
  5. Oceal Creamer’s dates, burial, and obituary.
  6. Obituaries or vital records that would confirm Thedra Fowler’s and Betty Lou Ausland’s dates independently of Find a Grave.
  7. The exact place of death, beyond the obituary’s “of Panama City.”
  8. The wording on her marker at Evergreen Memorial Gardens. The public memorial is linked; the inscription was not transcribed here.
  9. Family photographs, and memories the family is willing to have written down.
